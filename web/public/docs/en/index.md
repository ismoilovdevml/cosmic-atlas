## How we measure the universe

- [Parallax](doc:parallax): distances to stars by pure geometry.
- [Magnitude](doc:magnitude): how astronomers measure brightness.
- [Redshift](doc:redshift): stretched light that tells us how far galaxies are.
- [Lookback time](doc:lookback-time): looking far away means looking into the past.
- [Distance uncertainty](doc:distance-uncertainty): why every distance has a range, and how the atlas shows it.
- [Gaia](doc:gaia-mission): the spacecraft that measured nearly two billion stars.
- [DESI](doc:desi-survey): the instrument that mapped millions of galaxies in 3D.
- [Cosmic web](doc:cosmic-web): the giant network of galaxies and voids.

## Stars and what they leave behind

- [Star](doc:star): balls of hot gas powered by nuclear fusion.
- [Star cluster](doc:star-cluster): stars born together.
- [Nebula](doc:nebula): clouds of gas and dust, nurseries and graves of stars.
- [White dwarf](doc:white-dwarf): the Earth-sized ember of a Sun-like star.
- [Supernova remnant](doc:supernova-remnant): the cloud left by an exploded star.
- [Neutron star](doc:neutron-star): a star's core crushed to the size of a city.
- [Pulsar](doc:pulsar): spinning neutron stars that tick like clocks.
- [Black hole](doc:black-hole): where not even light can escape.
- [Exoplanet](doc:exoplanet): planets around other stars.
